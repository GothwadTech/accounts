Section 1

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

Gothwad Accounts is the central authentication and identity hub developed and operated by Gothwad Tech. Our vision is to empower Indian and global internet users with sovereign, secure, and user-centric digital tools under India's Atmanirbhar Bharat spirit.

💡
In Plain English

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers. We do not build shadow profiles or track you across the web. Your single Gothwad identity exists solely to let you securely sign into your Gothwad applications.

By signing up for or using a Gothwad Account, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Section 2

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization: we collect only what is strictly necessary to provide reliable authentication and security.

👤

Identity & Profile

Your chosen username (e.g. username which creates username@gothwadtech.com), display name, and optional date of birth.

🔑

Account Security Data

Salted cryptographic password hashes (we never store plain-text passwords), optional recovery email, and recovery phone number.

📱

Device & Session Info

Browser User-Agent, IP address (for approximate city/country location detection and fraud alerting), and session timestamps.

🔗

OAuth Authorizations

Records of applications you explicitly authorized via "Sign in with Gothwad", along with granted permission scopes.

Section 3

3. Why We Collect It

We use the information we collect for the following concrete purposes:

  • Provide Single Sign-On (SSO): Allowing you to sign in once and effortlessly access Gothwad Mail, ClashDrive, GrixChat, Notes, and Calendar without repeatedly entering credentials.
  • Protect against account takeover & abuse: Detecting credential stuffing, brute-force attempts, and suspicious logins from unrecognized devices.
  • Account recovery: Helping you securely reset your password via your verified recovery email or phone number if you forget it.
  • OAuth 2.0 Identity Provider: Providing trusted consent flows so third-party or internal apps can verify your identity without ever seeing your password.

Section 5

5. Information Sharing & OAuth

We do not share your personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Gothwad Tech except in the following limited circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent (OAuth 2.0): When you click "Authorize" on the Gothwad OAuth consent screen, we only share the specific profile attributes you approved (e.g. your username and email) with that application.
  • Legal obligations: When required by applicable Indian laws, lawful court orders, or enforceable governmental requests.
  • System safety: To detect, prevent, or address fraud, technical issues, or security threats to the Gothwad network.
Section 6

6. Security & Storage Architecture

Gothwad Accounts is built with enterprise-grade security practices from the ground up:

  • TLS / HTTPS Encryption: All communications between your browser, our Cloudflare edge nodes, and database are encrypted in transit.
  • HttpOnly, Secure Cookies & PKCE: We use cryptographic Proof Key for Code Exchange (PKCE) for OAuth 2.0 and HttpOnly JWT cookies to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS) token theft.
  • Row Level Security (RLS): Our database enforces strict row-level isolation so users can only ever access their own profile and session data.
  • Zero Budget & Sovereign Infrastructure: Designed to run securely on distributed edge infrastructure with Indian data residency principles.
Section 7

7. Retaining & Deleting Your Information

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion or data export at any time. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile and active sessions are permanently expunged from our database.
  • All active OAuth refresh tokens and app authorizations are immediately revoked.
  • Associated ecosystem data (Mail, Drive, Chat) follows the respective application retention schedule.
